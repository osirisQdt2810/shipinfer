# The two gates before the expensive part, instant by instant. Read by both planes.
#
#   scenario <name> <min_hits> <min_height_fraction>
#   instant  <camera>#<track>:<box_height_px> ...      (frames are 1920x1080 throughout)
#   instant  <camera>                                  a camera that reported and SAW NOTHING
#
# A bare camera name joins the instant's roster with no observation of its own. That is the
# one thing the format could not say before, and the difference the gate turns on: a camera
# that was there and saw nothing breaks its tracks' streaks, one that never landed says
# nothing about them.
#
# Heights are pixels of a 1080-tall frame so the fraction is readable: 200 is well over a
# ninth, 50 is well under, and 120 is exactly 1/9 of 1080 -- which is the boundary the
# comparison's strictness decides.

# Three consecutive qualifying frames, then admitted every frame after.
scenario min_hits_3 3 0.1111111111111111
instant cam0#1:200
instant cam0#1:200
instant cam0#1:200
instant cam0#1:200

# HEIGHT FIRST, THEN AGE: five frames too small bank no age at all, so the track still needs
# its three qualifying frames once it comes close. Swap the order and it would be admitted on
# the first usable frame.
scenario too_small_banks_nothing 3 0.1111111111111111
instant cam0#1:50
instant cam0#1:50
instant cam0#1:50
instant cam0#1:50
instant cam0#1:50
instant cam0#1:200
instant cam0#1:200
instant cam0#1:200

# AN INSTANT ITS CAMERA WAS NOT IN IS NOT A MISS. At fifty cameras an instant holds about a
# quarter of the fleet, so treating absence as a miss makes three consecutive sightings a 1.4%
# event -- measured, and it is what collapsed admission to 2.2%. cam0 is absent for one instant
# and its run survives, so the fourth line is its third SIGHTING and it is admitted.
scenario absence_carries_the_run 3 0.1111111111111111
instant cam0#1:200
instant cam0#1:200
instant cam1#9:200
instant cam0#1:200

# AND AN EMPTY VIEW IS NOT AN ABSENCE. cam0 reported the third instant and saw nothing, so it
# WAS there and its track was not: the run restarts and the fourth line is only hit 1. Without
# the roster these two scenarios are indistinguishable, which is the defect this format change
# exists to make reachable.
scenario an_empty_view_restarts_the_run 3 0.1111111111111111
instant cam0#1:200
instant cam0#1:200
instant cam0
instant cam0#1:200

# Strictly greater than the threshold: 120 of 1080 is exactly 1/9 and is OUT.
scenario boundary 1 0.1111111111111111
instant cam0#1:120
instant cam0#2:121

# The admitted keep their INPUT order, because the caller zips them against the labels.
scenario order 1 0.1111111111111111
instant cam2#1:200 cam0#1:200 cam1#1:200
